### Proof sources and the model's contribution

The run derived and locally checked a solution. The numerical search used the [public non-optimal illustration](https://pikaaa345.github.io/disk-covering-benchmark/assets/n27.svg). ### Task and acceptance standard

The n=27 task required the radius minimal polynomial with a rational isolating interval, exact centers, coverage of the closed disk and a global lower bound. The test allowed public sources and excluded local skills and pre-existing local materials outside its workspace.
